Glossary

What all those brackets and keywords in a card's skill text actually mean.

Ability Types

[AUTO] — Automatic Ability
Triggers on its own the moment its condition is met, like "when this unit attacks" or "when this card is ridden."
[CONT] — Continuous Ability
Passively active for as long as its condition holds true — there's no timing to activate, it just works.
[ACT] — Activate Ability
You choose when to use it, usually during your own main phase, often for a cost.
[COST]
What you have to pay right after an ability's timing to actually use it — discarding a card, Soul-Blasting, and so on.

Zones

G Zone
A separate zone holding your G units face-down until you Stride one of them onto your vanguard.
Damage Zone
Where cards go when you take damage. Face-up cards here can be spent to pay Counter-Blast costs.
Drop Zone
The discard pile — cards end up here when discarded, retired, or spent on most costs.
Bind Zone
A zone certain effects remove units to — cards sent here are usually much harder to bring back into play.

Actions & Resources

Ride / Ride Line
Placing a higher-grade unit on top of your vanguard, following your deck's grade progression (G0 → G1 → G2 → G3).
Stride
A special ride for your vanguard using a G unit from the G Zone as cost — gives a big power boost plus a bonus ability for the turn.
Generation Break X
A condition that's active once you've Strided X or more times so far this game.
Drive Check / Twin Drive / Triple Drive
When your vanguard attacks, you check cards off the top of your deck (2 for Twin Drive, 3 for Triple Drive) looking for triggers.
Intercept
A rear-guard moving in to guard your vanguard against an attack, usually retiring afterward as the cost.
Soul-Blast X
Cost: remove X cards from your Soul (the zone units go to when replaced by a ride).
Soul-Charge X
Put the top X cards of your deck into your Soul.
Counter-Blast X
Cost: turn X face-up cards in your Damage Zone face-down.

Card Roles

Vanguard
Your main battling unit, standing in the center of the field.
Rear-guard
Support units in the back row that boost or add extra attacks.
Sentinel
A trigger unit type that can guard the vanguard on its own, without first needing to be a rear-guard.
Grade (G0–G3, G unit)
A card's level. You ride in increasing grade order; G units (Stride units) sit outside that progression, waiting in the G Zone.

Each card has two tiles: its art, and a line from its skill. Match the art to the skill it belongs to — a good way to actually learn what your cards do.
